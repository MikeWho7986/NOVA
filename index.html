Nova

Neural Overseer & Virtual Assistant - a personal assistant for one person.

Nova is a private assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a public service: no one else can sign up or use it.

With the owner's permission, Nova has read-only access to the owner's Google account so it can:

  • read the owner's recent email to prepare a morning briefing and answer the owner's questions,
  • read the owner's Google Calendar and Google Tasks to say what's coming up.

Nova cannot send, change or delete anything in the Google account. Everything it reads stays on the owner's computer.
